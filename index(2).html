ABOUT UNLOCKED ARC

UNLOCKED ARC is a worldwide story project built around one simple belief: every life carries an untold arc.

We look for the human moments behind the surface — the struggle, decision, loss, hope, recovery, faith, change and quiet victories that shape who people become.

Stories shared with permission can be developed into meaningful cinematic and animated content, giving one person's experience the possibility of reaching someone who needs to hear it.

01 · Human Real experiences come first.
02 · Respect Stories are handled with dignity.
